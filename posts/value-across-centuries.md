## Art corner: The Night Watch

Rembrandt's *Night Watch* is cataloged by the Rijksmuseum under object number **SK-C-5**, dated **1642**.

- **Title:** *Het korporaalschap van kapitein Frans Banninck Cocq en luitenant Willem van Ruytenburch, bekend als de 'Nachtwacht'* (The Militia Company of Captain Frans Banninck Cocq and Lieutenant Willem van Ruytenburch, known as the 'Night Watch')
- **Object number:** SK-C-5
- **Year:** 1642
- **Materials:** oil on canvas
- **Dimensions:** approximately 379.5 cm × 359.5 cm (h × w)
- **Commission:** commissioned by Captain Frans Banninck Cocq and Lieutenant Willem van Ruytenburch of the Klovenarsdoelen (Artists' Guild) militia company; the painting was long thought to depict a night scene but is actually set in daylight, with the "night" appearance resulting from dark varnish over the centuries.

Record ID: https://id.rijksmuseum.nl/200107928
