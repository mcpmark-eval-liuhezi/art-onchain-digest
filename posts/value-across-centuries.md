## Art corner: Rembrandt's Night Watch

The museum catalogs the masterpiece under **object number SK-C-5**:

- **Title:** *Het korporaalschap van kapitein Frans Banninck Cocq en luitenant Willem van Ruytenburch, bekend als de 'Nachtwacht'* (The Night Watch)
- **Year:** 1642
- **Materials:** Oil on canvas
- **Dimensions:** Approximately 379.5 cm × 479.5 cm (height × width) — one of the largest paintings in the Rijksmuseum's collection
- **Location:** Rijksmuseum, Amsterdam

## Rembrandt's career chronology (Rijksmuseum records)

Dating his output across the museum's collection traces a remarkable arc from early Leiden years to late Amsterdam masterpieces:

- **1626** — *Allegorical Music Company* (SK-A-4674): an early work showing the youthful Rembrandt's talent for History painting.
- **1634** — *Portrait of Haesje Jacobsdr van Cleyburg* (SK-A-4833): a commissioned Amsterdam portrait from the early 1630s.
- **1635** — *An Oriental* (SK-A-3340): a study in Orientalist-type portraiture from the middle of the 1630s.
- **1638** — *De stenen brug* (The Stone Bridge, SK-A-1935): a landscape from the late 1630s.
- **1642** — *The Night Watch* (SK-C-5): the grande史上 Picture of his Amsterdam years, carved in 1642.
- **1660** — *The Denial of Saint Peter* (SK-A-3137) and *Rembrandt's son in a monk's habit* (SK-A-3138, possibly St Francis of Assisi).
- **1662** — *The Sampling Officials* (De Staalmeesters, SK-C-6): the ambitious group portrait of the Amsterdam drapers' guild committee.
- **1665** — *The Jewish Bride* (SK-C-216): one of Rembrandt's late masterworks,ocytingly near-monumental in its simplified, expressive brushwork.
