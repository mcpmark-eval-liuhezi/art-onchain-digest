## Art corner: The Night Watch

- **Object number:** SK-C-5
- **Year:** 1642
- **Title (museum catalog):** Het korporaalschap van kapitein Frans Banninck Cocq en luitenant Willem van Ruytenburch, bekend als de 'Nachtwacht' (The Night Watch)
- **Materials:** oil on canvas
- **Dimensions:** approx. 379.5 cm (h) × 479.5 cm (w) — one of the largest paintings in the Rijksmuseum collection
- **Current location:** Rijksmuseum, Amsterdam

The museum's collection record confirms object number SK-C-5 and the 1642 date. The work is catalogued as an oil-on-canvas militia company portrait, and although it is universally known as "The Night Watch," the nickname arose only after the varnish darkened over centuries; the scene is actually set in daylight.

## Rembrandt's Career: A Chronology from the Museum's Data

From the Rijksmuseum's collection data, Rembrandt's dated works span four decades:

- **1626 — SK-A-4674, *Allegorisch musicerend gezelschap* (Allegorical company making music)** — early Leiden-period work, dated 1626.
- **1634 — SK-A-4833, *Portrait of Haesje Jacobsdr van Cleyburg*** — commissioned portrait of the wife of the Rotterdam brewer Dirck Jansz Pesser, dated 1634.
- **1638 — SK-A-1935, *De stenen brug* (The Stone Bridge)** — landscape dated 1638.
- **1642 — SK-C-5, *The Night Watch*** — the masterpiece at the heart of this issue, dated 1642.
- **1660 — SK-A-3137, *De verlouchening van Petrus* (The Denial of Saint Peter)* and SK-A-3138, *Rembrandt's son in a monk's habit, possibly as St Francis of Assisi*** — late works from 1660.
- **1662 — SK-C-6, *De Staalmeesters* (The Sampling Officials / College of the Worstervers of the Amsterdam Drapers' Guild)** — dated 1662.
- **1665 — SK-C-216, *The Jewish Bride* (Portrait of a couple as figures from the Old Testament, known as 'The Jewish Bride')** — one of Rembrandt's late masterworks, dated 1665.
