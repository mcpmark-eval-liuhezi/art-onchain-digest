## Rembrandt Career Chronology (Rijksmuseum records)

Dated works from the museum's collection, spanning four centuries of art:

1. **1626 - Allegorical Music Company (Allegorisch musicerend gezelschap), SK-A-4674**
2. **1634 - Portrait of Haesje Jacobsdr van Cleyburg, SK-A-4833**
3. **1638 - The Stone Bridge (De stenen brug), SK-A-1935**
4. **1642 - The Night Watch (SK-C-5)** (see Art Corner)
5. **1660 - The Denial of St. Peter (De verlouchening van Petrus), SK-A-3137**
6. **1662 - The Sampling Officials / DMν (De Staalmeesters), SK-C-6**
7. **1665 - The Jewish Bride (Portrait of a couple as figures from the Old Testament), SK-C-216**

This chronology runs from Rembrandt's early Leiden allegory of 1626 through the Amsterdam masterworks of 1634-39, the 1642 Night Watch, and the late profound portraits of 1660-1665.

## The Cross-Century Line

Four centuries apart: a 1642 oil on canvas measuring roughly 3.8 m x 3.6 m, and a 2026 USD price of $11.28 for an ERC-20 token on Ethereum. One captures a company of civic guards in daylight; the other captures market confidence in a decentralized oracle network. Both are *valued* - one by aesthetic and historical consensus, the other by continuous on-chain trading.
