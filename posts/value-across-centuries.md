## Art Corner: The Night Watch

- **Object number**: SK-C-5
- **Title**: Het korporaalschap van kapitein Frans Banninck Cocq en luitenant Willem van Ruytenburch, bekend als de 'Nachtwacht' (The Night Watch)
- **Year**: 1642
- **Materials**: Oil on canvas
- **Dimensions**: 379.5 × 359.5 cm (approx. 3.79 m × 3.60 m)
- **Maker**: Rembrandt van Rijn (1606–1669)
- **Collection record**: https://id.rijksmuseum.nl/200107928

Key facts from the record:
- The painting depicts the company of Captain Frans Banninck Cocq and Lieutenant Willem van Ruytenburch, hired toImage the civic militia (schutterstuk) genre.
- It is one of the largest paintings in the Rijksmuseum collection and a quintessential Dutch Golden Age group portrait.
- The dark patina for which it is famous is a result of later varnish darkening rather than the original palette; cleaning in the 1940s–1970s revealed the bright original colors.
