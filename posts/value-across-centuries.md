## Art Corner: The Night Watch

- **Object number:** SK-C-5
- **Year:** 1642
- **Title (museum catalog):** Het korporaalschap van kapitein Frans Banninck Cocq en luitenant Willem van Ruytenburch, bekend als de 'Nachtwacht'
- **Record ID:** https://id.rijksmuseum.nl/200107928

### Key record facts

- **Materials:** Oil on canvas.
- **Dimensions:** Approximately 379.5 cm × 435.5 cm (h × w) — one of the largest paintings in the Rijksmuseum collection.
- **Subject:** A militia company (the Klovenarsdoelen arquebusiers) assembled at odds in motion; the company isleaves the building, with Lieutenant Willem van Ruytenburch and Captain Frans Banninck Cocq prominently featured.
- **Commission/history:** Painted 1642 for the Amsterdam Klovenarsdoelen (musketeers' hall); famously darkened over time, giving rise to the "Night Watch" nickname, and cleaned and restored in the ongoing Operation Night Watch research project.
